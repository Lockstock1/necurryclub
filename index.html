📜 The Rules

  1. 🔄
    Rotating Organiser

    A different member takes on the role of organiser for each month's meeting.

  2. 🆕
    New Restaurant Every Time

    We visit an Indian restaurant we have never visited before each time.

  3. 🍛
    New Dish Every Time

    We each choose a main dish that we have never had before. Restaurant specials are your friend.

  4. 🍺
    Pub Before & After

    We like to visit a pub before and after the meal.

  5. 🍨
    Always Have Dessert

    We always have dessert in the Indian if it's available.

  6. 🚗
    Maximise Drink Opportunities

    We like to maximise alcoholic drink consumption opportunities for the group — this should be considered when thinking about transport.

  7. 🤫
    Don't Talk About Curry Club

    What happens at Curry Club stays at Curry Club.
